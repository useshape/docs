Shape routes AI Chat through cloud models. A Shape account is required to use any cloud model. Every plan includes **Auto**, Shape's built-in routing to a fast backend model. Paid plans unlock the full model catalog, billed in credits.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Credits / month | Models available |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Auto allowance only | Auto only |
| **Plus** | $15 / mo | 450 credits | Auto + all premium |
| **Pro** | $50 / mo | 1,500 credits | Auto + all premium |
| **Max** | $150 / mo | 4,500 credits | Auto + all premium |

You can check your current usage from the avatar menu in the app.

## Auto Model

**Auto** is Shape's included tier — it routes your messages to a fast, capable backend model and doesn't draw from your credit balance. Free accounts use Auto exclusively. Plus and higher plans can use Auto alongside any premium model.

Start with Auto before reaching for a premium model. It handles the majority of everyday coding tasks well.

## Tips for Managing Credits

<Tip>
Use **Ask mode** when you don't need file edits. Ask mode still reads your codebase, but read-only conversations are typically shorter and cheaper than agentic Code mode sessions.
</Tip>

- **Start with Auto** before switching to a premium model — you may not need the upgrade
- **Disable models you don't use** in **Settings → Agents → Enabled models** to keep the picker clean
- Long conversations accumulate context. Starting a new thread for a new task keeps token counts lower
- When you reach your monthly credit limit, upgrade your plan or wait for the next monthly reset
